# How to Get Started with Table Tennis Doubles

## About the Project

This project is a simple HTML blog that introduces beginners to table tennis doubles. It explains the basic skills and techniques needed to enjoy the game, including serving, staying ready, returning the ball, teamwork, and staying focused.

The blog also shares the importance of teamwork, patience, and sportsmanship in playing table tennis doubles.

## Topics Covered

1. **Learn How to Serve** – Understand the proper way to serve in doubles.
2. **Stay Low and Ready** – Learn how to prepare your body for the next shot.
3. **Practice Your Return** – Improve ball control and practice returning shots.
4. **Take Turns With Your Partner** – Understand teamwork and proper movement.
5. **Stay Focused and Enjoy the Game** – Learn to stay calm, play fairly, and enjoy the match.

## Purpose

This blog aims to help beginners understand the basic rules and techniques of table tennis doubles while encouraging teamwork, focus, and enjoyment of the sport.
